OUR POLICY

Return & Exchange Policy

We want you to love your purchase! However, if you receive a damaged or incorrect item, we offer a hassle-free return/exchange policy under the following terms:

  • 2-Day Return Window: You must request a return or exchange within 2 days of delivery.
  • Mandatory Unboxing Video: A 360-degree clear, unedited, and continuous unboxing video from start to finish is strictly required to claim any return or damage request.
  • No Edits or Cuts: Videos with cuts, edits, pauses, or recorded after the package is already opened will not be accepted.
  • Condition: The product must be unused, unworn, and in its original packaging with all tags intact.

To initiate a return, please share your order details along with the unboxing video with us on WhatsApp.
